## What we collect

### If you ask to be told about a release

- **What:** your email address, and the date you submitted it.
- **Why:** to send you a single message when the first release is published, and nothing else.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (KVKK art. 5/1; GDPR art. 6(1)(a)).
- **How long:** until you unsubscribe, or {{RETENTION_PERIOD}}, whichever comes first.
- **Who else sees it:** {{EMAIL_PROCESSOR_OR_NONE}}.

### Server logs

- **What:** IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamp, recorded by the hosting provider.
- **Why:** to keep the site running and to detect abuse.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)); KVKK art. 5/2(f).
- **How long:** {{LOG_RETENTION}}.
- **Processor:** {{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}, {{HOSTING_LOCATION}}.

## Your rights

Under **KVKK art. 11** and the **GDPR (arts. 15–22)** you may request access to your data, correction,
erasure, restriction, portability, and you may object to processing or withdraw consent at any time.
Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.

To exercise any of these, write to **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**. We respond within **30 days**.

You may also complain to a supervisory authority — in Türkiye, the **Personal Data Protection Authority
(KVKK Kurumu)**; in the EEA, your national data protection authority.
